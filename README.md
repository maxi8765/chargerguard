# Charger Guard

A free Android app that sounds an alarm when you walk away and leave your phone charger behind.

Plug a Feasycom FSC-BP101E USB Bluetooth beacon into your charger's spare USB port. While your phone charges, Charger Guard hears the beacon and arms itself. If you unplug and walk away without the charger, the alarm goes off once the beacon has been out of range for a few seconds.

Website: https://maxi8765.github.io/chargerguard/

Download: [ChargerGuard.apk](https://maxi8765.github.io/chargerguard/ChargerGuard.apk) (version 1.0.0). Android 8 or later.
